Zone Architecture
Zone 1: Entrance Portal (RFID + IR Door)
Zone 2: Ceiling & Appliances (LCD, Fan, LEDs)
Zone 3: Control Brain (ESP32, Relay, DHT22)
Component Name

ESP32 DevKit V1 (30-pin)

Master Controller

Optimal Spatial Placement

Mounted on the 30-pin expansion breakout board inside the main control enclosure (Zone 3), placed at the bottom-right corner of the prototype baseboard.

Why This Placement Is Most Efficient

Centrally positions the microcontroller within 15–20cm of all sensors and actuators. Keeps SPI bus for RFID short while isolating the ESP32 logic from high-current relay switching.

Hardware Connections

VIN → 5V GND → Common GND 3V3 → 3.3V Rail

Engineering Advice & Mistakes to Avoid

Ensure correct antenna orientation facing outwards away from metal/enclosure walls to maintain maximum 2.4GHz Wi-Fi reception for Firebase Realtime Database updates.
